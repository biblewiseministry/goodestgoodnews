## Full Description

Use this text for both the App Store (iOS) and Google Play long description.
Plain text only — no markdown. Adjust line breaks to suit each store's preview formatting.

---

```
The Goodest Good News is a beautifully illustrated digital storybook that brings the Gospel to life for young children.

Designed for ages 3–7 and perfect for bedtime, quiet time, or family devotionals, the app lets children hear and follow the story of God's love in a warm, engaging way.

READ TO ME
Tap "Read to Me" and listen as each page spread is narrated aloud. Follow along with the illustrations at your own pace. Tap Replay to hear a page again, or move forward and back whenever you're ready.

READ BY MYSELF
Tap "Read by Myself" to explore the story independently. All the same beautiful spreads, without narration — perfect for early readers or children who want to go at their own pace.

SIMPLE AND DISTRACTION-FREE
No ads. No in-app purchases. No account required. No auto-play. No background music. Just the story, designed to be calm and focused for young readers.

SAFE FOR YOUNG CHILDREN
The app collects no personal information and requires no internet connection to use. It is fully compliant with children's privacy regulations (COPPA).

THE STORY
From creation to the cross to new life — The Goodest Good News tells the big story of the Bible in language a child can understand and a parent will love to share.

Illustrated with care. Narrated with warmth. Designed for families.
```
