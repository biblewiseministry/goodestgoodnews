# Screenshot Plan — The Goodest Good News

---

## Overview

Both the App Store and Google Play require screenshots for submission. Because this app requires landscape orientation for the story, all screenshots should be captured in landscape on a real device or simulator.

**Capture device recommendation:** iPad (for App Store — required) and iPhone 6.9" / 6.5" (required sizes). Android: tablet and phone.

---

## Required Screenshot Sizes (iOS App Store)

| Device | Size | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 6.9" (iPhone 15 Pro Max) | 1320 x 2868 px (portrait canvas, landscape content) | Yes |
| iPhone 6.5" (iPhone 14 Plus / 11 Pro Max) | 1242 x 2688 px | Yes |
| iPad Pro 13" | 2048 x 2732 px | Yes (if supportsTablet: true) |
| iPad Pro 12.9" (older) | 2048 x 2732 px | Optional |

**Note:** Apple accepts screenshots in landscape orientation even though the device frame is portrait. You can also present landscape screenshots as-is without a device frame.

## Google Play Screenshot Requirements

- Minimum: 2 screenshots
- Recommended: 4–8
- Minimum size: 320px on shortest side, max 3840px on longest side
- Use the same landscape screenshots as iOS where possible

---

## Capture Tips

- Use a physical device if possible — the screen looks better than a simulator
- Capture at full resolution (do not scale down before uploading)
- Disable the status bar clock if possible, or use a clean time (e.g., 9:41 AM)
- Turn off notifications before capturing
- Capture in landscape; the story requires it and screenshots will look natural
- No need for device frames if the content fills the screen cleanly
