The Goodest Good News

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026
Developer: Erik Meyers

Overview

The Goodest Good News is a digital storybook app designed for young children and families. It presents an illustrated, narrated story in a simple, self-contained format. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

What This App Does Not Do

  • No account required. The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it.
  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your child.
  • No tracking. The app does not track your behavior, location, or usage patterns. No analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • No advertising. The app contains no advertisements of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases. The app contains no paid upgrades, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
  • No social features. The app has no social sharing, chat, comments, or community features.
  • No internet connection required. All story content, images, and narration audio are stored locally on your device. The app does not make network requests during normal use.
  • No microphone use. The app plays pre-recorded narration audio. It does not record audio or access the microphone.
  • No camera use. The app does not access the device camera.
  • No third-party SDKs for data collection. The app does not include any advertising networks, analytics services, or data brokers.

What the App Does

The app provides:

  • A splash screen and dedication screen
  • A home screen to choose a reading mode
  • Story Mode with page-by-page illustrated spreads
  • "Read to Me" mode with pre-recorded narration audio
  • "Read by Myself" mode without narration
  • Page navigation controls (Previous, Next, Home, Replay)

All content is static and pre-loaded. Nothing is sent to or received from any server during use.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for young children to use with their families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. No parental consent is required because no data is collected.

Changes to This Policy

If the app is updated in a future version to include features that affect privacy (such as cloud saves or optional accounts), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
